## How to start a project

1. Book a free discovery call or send the contact form.
2. Tell us the workflow, the tools you already use, and the outcome you want.
3. You receive a written summary and a quote before we write code.
4. Engagement options: a dedicated developer in your team, a full project, or a fixed-scope first version.

Staff augmentation means one of our developers works in your team, full-time or part-time, under your direction. Outsourcing means we take responsibility for the whole project, from planning to delivery.

To hire a dedicated developer: book a free call, share your tech stack and requirements. We shortlist developers, you interview them, and once you choose one, they join your daily workflow.

## Delivery process

01 Understand: We look at how your work runs today, which tools you use and where the time or money goes. You receive a short written summary of the problem and your priorities.
02 Design: We design the solution before writing code. You receive a clickable prototype or architecture document, and a fixed quote or clear rate.
03 Build: We build in weekly cycles. You receive a working build every week and a short progress note.

05 Launch and support: We release the product, watch how it performs and keep improving it. You receive a live product, handover documents and a support plan.

Custom AI agents that answer customers, handle routine requests and complete multi-step work in your existing tools. Each agent has clear rules and a log of every action.

A first agent pilot for one workflow is often 2 to 4 weeks after we agree the tools, rules and success check.

Write-back to CRM or sending email happens only if we agree that in the spec. The default is drafts and suggestions that a person approves.

To start we need the workflow owner, the tools in use, and examples of good and bad outcomes. Discovery is free.

## Free agents catalogue

Lead Qualification Agent: Score inbound leads, ask the next useful question and hand off qualified conversations. Capabilities: lead scoring, CRM notes after approval, handoff summaries, human review gates. It does not close or discard leads.

Customer Support Agent: Answer common questions from your knowledge base and escalate when a human is needed. Capabilities: knowledge answers from approved articles, ticket classification, escalation pack, tone controls. It does not invent policy or discounts.

Meeting Summary Agent: Turn meeting notes into action items, owners and a short recap. Capabilities: action items, decision log, owner extraction from names already in the notes, shareable recap.

Document Extraction Agent: Pull structured fields from invoices, forms and PDFs. Capabilities: field extraction, document classification, export JSON/CSV, review queue for low-confidence fields.

Sales Research Agent: Prepare a concise brief on a prospect from public context. Capabilities: account briefs, talking points labelled as suggestions, source list, CRM-ready notes after a person checks them.

Data Reporting Agent: Recurring operational snapshot in plain language. Capabilities: metric recap, trend notes without inventing a cause, scheduled summaries, drill-down only from the connected metric set.

Content Brief Agent: Structured content brief from a topic, audience and offer. Capabilities: brief outline, audience notes, proof points that still need a source, SEO questions. It does not auto-publish copy or fabricate stats.

Email Response Agent: Suggest replies for common inbound emails. Nothing sends until a person approves.

Website FAQ Agent: Site assistant grounded in published FAQs and help articles. If the answer is not on those pages, it points to contact. It does not crawl the open web.

Research Agent: Collect public positioning notes so strategy conversations start from a shared brief. Gaps are labelled as unknown, not guessed. Research, not legal advice.

Are these agents actually free? Yes, to try. Custom wiring into live systems is quoted separately. Drafts, scores and summaries wait for a person unless a specific write-back is agreed in a custom build.
